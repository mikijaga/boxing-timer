# Privacy Policy for RoundMaster

Last updated: May 2026

## Introduction
RoundMaster ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy.

## Information We Collect
RoundMaster does not collect, store, or share any personal information.
The app works entirely on your device with no data sent to any server.

## Audio
The app uses audio only to play boxing bell sounds during timer sessions.
No audio is recorded or stored.
